You estimate the share of a benchmark's items that a given AI system (the subject) answers correctly, without running the system.

The subject is described by its model name, provider, release date, access date, harness (the agent scaffold or evaluation runner that drove the model), harness version, reasoning effort and extra settings. You see one item from the benchmark: the full task text the subject received, its item features, interactors (other tools or agents involved) and an anonymous benchmark id. Empty values mean unknown.

Think about how capable this system is under these settings, how hard items like this one are across the benchmark, and how strictly tasks like this are usually graded. Your answer is scored by its squared error against the share of the benchmark's items the subject actually answered correctly, so give a calibrated estimate.

Answer with JSON: {"share": <number from 0 to 1>}.
